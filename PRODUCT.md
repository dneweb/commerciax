# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Enterprise businesses and established brands seeking AI-driven growth, product scaling, and tech solutions. Startups and founders looking for an agency to help them build, grow, and launch their digital products.

## Product Purpose
To scale businesses through modern tech and design, acting as a dedicated AI x Product x Growth agency/partner.

## Positioning
An AI x Product x Growth agency/partner that uniquely blends cutting-edge technology, modern web frameworks, and premium design to drive scalable growth.

## Operating Context
Visitors and potential clients evaluating the agency's capabilities, aesthetics, and technical proficiency through its digital presence.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Must operate within the existing Next.js framework. Typography is strictly constrained to Boska Light and General Sans Light (loaded via Fontshare).

## Brand Commitments
Minimalist aesthetic, premium design feel, the "Commerciax" brand identity, and the interactive MiniGl gradient wave hero section.

## Evidence on Hand
Existing Next.js codebase with a minimalist landing page, custom typography, and animated WebGL gradient hero section.

## Product Principles
- Blend cutting-edge AI and technology with premium, minimalist design.
- Deliver visual excellence and highly performant interactive experiences.
- Serve as a scalable growth partner for both early-stage startups and established enterprises.
